# EvidenceRelay Band Pitch Deck

Editable PPTX: `docs/evidencerelay-band-pitch.pptx`

## Slide 1: EvidenceRelay Band

Verifier-gated multi-agent incident response for high-stakes teams, with four agents, five room messages, two verified findings, and one human approval gate.

## Slide 2: Problem

Fast incident-response agents are useful, but ungated agents are risky when confirmed claims are not traceable to evidence, tool calls, and integrity state.

## Slide 3: Room Workflow

EvidenceLock bundle -> Triage Agent -> Verifier Agent -> Response Lead -> Comms Agent, with the verifier gate before response planning.

## Slide 4: Product Proof

The generated transcript contains evidence references, tool references, message types, mentions, and a human approval flag.

## Slide 5: Architecture

The simulator uses an isolated room adapter. After lablab/Band access exists, that transport can be replaced with a real Band room/message adapter while preserving the verifier contract.

## Slide 6: Evaluator Proof Pack

Public repo, public demo, local tests, cover image, judge pack, video script, editable deck, simulator transcript, and static judge page.

## Slide 7: Differentiator

EvidenceRelay Band optimizes for accountable autonomy: collaboration is allowed, unsupported action is not.

## Slide 8: Submission Status

Public assets are ready. Remaining gates are lablab/Band enrollment, real room/API proof, video upload, slide upload, and final lablab submission.
